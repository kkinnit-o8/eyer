Eyer

Games you play with your eyes.

  • Ball: look at the red ball and it jumps somewhere else. Catch as many as you can in 15 seconds.
  • Peripheral rhythm: play any osu! map on one key (or as 4-key mania) while your eyes pop hit circles in the corners. The notes are only in the corner of your eye.
  • Your webcam tracks your eyes. The video stays on your device.
  • Light your face from the front and sit 40–60 cm from the screen.
  • Keep your head fairly still and move your eyes.

Tip 1 of 4

Set up your space

  • Light in front of you. A lamp or window behind your screen, not behind you. Dim light or light from behind makes your eyes hard to see.
  • Camera at eye level, about an arm's length away. On a laptop, tilt the screen until your face is in the middle of the preview.
  • Keep your eyes clear. No glare on glasses, and no hair or cap shading your eyes.

Calibrate with your eyes, not your head

Should I move my head? No. Keep your head still and move only your eyes.

  • The tracker learns how your irises move. If you turn your head toward each dot, it learns those head turns instead and can't tell where your eyes alone are looking.
  • Look at the white center of the dot and keep looking until it has shrunk away. Don't jump ahead to where you think it goes next.
  • Keep a relaxed face. Squinting or a big smile changes the shape of your eyes. Blinking is fine; blinks are ignored.

Play the way you calibrated

  • Stay put. Calibration only fits the spot your head was in. Leaning in, sitting back or turning your head throws it off.
  • Let your eyes do the work. Flick them straight to the ball. It vanishes the moment the tracker sees you looking at it.
  • Recalibrate when something changes: you moved, the light changed, or the game left fullscreen. It only takes 20 seconds.

Check and fix

  • On the tracking check, look at each grey target. It turns red when the tracker agrees with you.
  • Ring off to the same side everywhere? Recalibrate, keeping your head still.
  • Ring shaky? Put more light on your face or move a little closer.
  • Up and down is harder than left and right for every webcam tracker. A camera at eye level helps most.
  • Press G in the game to see the ring while you play.

Peripheral rhythm

Notes fall down the middle, but you can't look at them. Hit circles land in the corners on the beat: look at each one to pop it before its timer runs out, or you lose your streak. Read the notes from the corner of your eye. Stare at the middle and the notes disappear.
